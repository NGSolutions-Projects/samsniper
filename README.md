# S.A.M Sniper

**S.A.M Sniper** is a Windows desktop application from **NG Solutions** that
helps small businesses find and work government contract opportunities - federal,
state and local - in one place.

> S.A.M Sniper is not publicly available at this time. It is provided by
> invitation. For access or questions, email **support@samsniper.com**.

## What it does

- **Federal Search** - searches SAM.gov's full daily file of open opportunities
  on your computer, with keyword, NAICS, PSC, set-aside and place filters, plus
  anything posted since last night.
- **State Search** - every open state and local bid for the states you choose,
  read from each state's official procurement sites, each linking back to its
  official page.
- **Fit scores** - every opportunity is scored against your company profile so
  the best matches come first; *Can we win it?* rates eligibility, capability,
  past performance, capacity, competition and timeline.
- **Pipeline** - track opportunities from lead to close, record replies, draft
  messages, and keep each notice's documents with it.
- **Grants, Recompetes, Teaming partners and Labor rates** - federal grants,
  contracts coming up for renewal, possible teaming partners from SAM.gov
  registrations, and GSA labor rates for pricing.
- **Compliance matrix and proposal outline** - every requirement in a
  solicitation, with the page it came from, exported to Excel and Word.
- **Exports, email digests and Slack / Teams alerts.**

## Privacy

Your searches, company profile, notes and documents stay on your computer.
The application talks only to the official sources it reads (such as SAM.gov,
Grants.gov and state procurement sites) and to services you choose to connect.
There is no analytics or tracking. Optional AI help uses your own Anthropic key,
shows exactly what will be sent before anything is sent, and never sends
documents marked CUI or export-controlled.

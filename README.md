# S.A.M. Sniper

**S.A.M. Sniper is a Windows desktop application from NG Solutions. It was
designed as an educational and research project to help organizations of
every size - small businesses, large firms, nonprofits and the teams that
support them - find, judge and win government contracts.**

## Most businesses think the hard part is the proposal

It isn't. The hard part is the opportunity you never saw.

Government buying doesn't happen in one place. Federal agencies post on
SAM.gov. Every state runs its own procurement system - often several - and
counties, cities, school districts, universities and transit authorities run
thousands more. Each has its own search, its own vocabulary and its own
schedule. Opportunities are amended, re-posted and closed every day.

So most businesses do what seems sensible: they watch the one or two sites
they know, search the same few keywords, and bid on what happens to surface.
Their competitors with dedicated capture teams see everything else.

**The firms that win government work consistently aren't always better at
the work. They're better at seeing it first, and at knowing which bids are
worth their time.**

## The size of what's being missed

| | FY 2024 | FY 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Federal contract spending | **$755 billion** | **$793 billion** |
| Prime contract dollars to small businesses | **$183.5 billion** (28.76%) | **~$179 billion** (~28%) |
| Small business total, prime + subcontracts | - | **~$273 billion** |
| Federal contract awards with activity in the year | **~5.9 million** | **~5.8 million** |
| ...of which held by small businesses | **~2.9 million** (about half) | **~2.9 million** (about half) |

And that's only the federal market. **State and local governments spend more
than $3.5 trillion a year** - spread across every state's own systems.

Small businesses aren't on the sidelines of this market. They hold about half
of all federal contract awards and earn more than a quarter of federal prime
contract dollars every year. The question isn't whether the opportunity
exists. It's whether **you** see it before the deadline - and whether you can
tell, quickly, which ones you can actually win.

## What it costs to keep doing it the old way

- **The deadline you found a day too late.** A strong fit, posted on a site you
  weren't watching, closed before you knew it existed.
- **The bid you shouldn't have chased.** Days of proposal effort on work you
  were never eligible for, or never positioned to win.
- **The amendment you missed.** The due date moved, a requirement changed, and
  your response went in against the old version.
- **The hours.** Someone on your team re-running the same searches across the
  same sites every morning, copying results into a spreadsheet.

Every one of those is revenue that went to someone else.

## A different way to work the market

Winning teams don't search harder. They change what they look at:

1. **One view of the whole market** - federal, state and local together, not a
   dozen tabs.
2. **Fit first** - every opportunity weighed against what *your* organization
   actually does, is certified for, and has done before.
3. **Nothing slips** - amendments, deadlines and next steps tracked from first
   sighting to final decision.
4. **Your data stays yours** - the work happens on your own computer.

## S.A.M. Sniper

S.A.M. Sniper brings that way of working to your desktop:

- **Sees the market for you** - official federal sources and a growing list of
  states' official procurement systems, gathered into one place and kept
  current.
- **Tells you what's worth your time** - opportunities are scored against your
  organization's profile, so the strongest fits rise to the top and the
  long shots are clearly marked.
- **Keeps you ahead of changes** - new postings and amendments are flagged, and
  tracked opportunities move with them.
- **Takes you from lead to decision** - a working pipeline for the bids you
  pursue, with the context you need to decide whether and how to respond.
- **Respects your privacy and the sources** - your searches, profile and notes
  stay on your computer; every listing credits and links back to its official
  source.

S.A.M. Sniper is built and refined as an ongoing research project in how
organizations can find and evaluate public-sector opportunities more fairly
and efficiently. It is offered by invitation while it grows.
